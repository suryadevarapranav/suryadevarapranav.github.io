## Hello, World!

Welcome to **Bits & Builds** — my little corner of the internet where I document what I'm building, what I'm learning, and everything in between.

I've always believed that the best way to learn is by doing, and the best way to solidify what you've learned is by sharing it. That's what this blog is all about.

## What to expect

Here's what you'll find on this blog:

- **Project walkthroughs** — I'll break down the things I build, from idea to implementation
- **Lessons learned** — The stuff that took me hours to figure out, distilled into something useful
- **Tech explorations** — Deep dives into tools, frameworks, and concepts that catch my attention
- **Tips & tricks** — Quick wins and shortcuts I pick up along the way

## Why "Bits & Builds"?

Because everything in tech comes down to bits — small pieces that come together to create something bigger. And I love building things. The name felt right.

## Let's go

This is just the beginning. If you're someone who likes to build, learn, and grow — stick around. There's a lot more to come.

Thanks for reading, and welcome aboard!

— Pranav
